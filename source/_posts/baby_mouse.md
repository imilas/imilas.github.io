I was shoveling in the backyard when the doctor brought me a mouse and released it next to my feet. The mouse ran off and crawled into a bucket. I grabbed the handle and set off to free him in a field nearby. I took a closer look as I walked: it was young and in rough shape, probably wouldn't survive. So I put it on the ground, smashed it with the shovel then buried it.
